Solar kWh estimate

Clear-sky annual output, computed in your browser by @pvkit/core: hourly NREL SPA → Ineichen clear sky → Perez POA → SAPM cell temperature → PVWatts DC, losses and inverter. No server.

Clear sky is an upper bound: clouds typically cut real yield by 20–40%. Months are local standard time approximated from longitude. For weather-driven estimates, see @pvkit/io.
