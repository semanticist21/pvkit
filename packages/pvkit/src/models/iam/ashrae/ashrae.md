# ashrae — ASHRAE transmission IAM

## Principle

`IAM = 1 − b·(1/cos aoi − 1)`, clamped to `max(0, ·)`; `|aoi| ≥ 90 → 0`. The model goes
negative near 90° (at `aoi ≈ 87.1°` for `b = 0.05`), hence the clamp. NaN aoi → NaN
(pvlib behaviour).

## Reference

- **Spec:** A. F. Souka, H. H. Safwat, "Determination of the optimum orientations for the
  double exposure flat-plate collector and its reflections", *Solar Energy* 10(4):170–174,
  1966, doi:10.1016/0038-092X(66)90004-1; ASHRAE Standard 93-77.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.iam.ashrae(aoi, b)` @ pvlib 0.16.1.
- **Fixtures:** `ashrae-fixtures.json` (87 cases: aoi sweep −135…180 incl. the 87° zero
  crossing and 90°, `b = 0` and `0.2`, 60 random), generated by `scripts/fixtures/iam-ashrae.py`.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-12`. Observed max error vs pvlib on V8: 0 (bit-identical); margin for
  cross-engine `Math.cos` ULP differences.
