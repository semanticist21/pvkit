# martin-ruiz — Martin & Ruiz IAM

## Principle

`IAM = (1 − exp(−cos aoi / a_r)) / (1 − exp(−1 / a_r))` (paper Eq. 3–4 as `AL = 1 − IAM`,
with the 2013 corrigendum's `a_r` notation). IAM(0) = 1; `|aoi| ≥ 90 → 0`. NaN aoi → NaN.
`aR ≤ 0` (or NaN) throws `RangeError` (pvlib raises `ValueError` for `a_r ≤ 0`; it lets
NaN through as NaN output).

## Reference

- **Spec:** N. Martin, J. M. Ruiz, "Calculation of the PV modules angular losses under field
  conditions by means of an analytical model", *Solar Energy Materials & Solar Cells*
  70(1):25–38, 2001, doi:10.1016/S0927-0248(00)00408-6; corrigendum *SEMSC* 110:154, 2013,
  doi:10.1016/j.solmat.2012.11.002.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.iam.martin_ruiz(aoi, a_r)` @ pvlib 0.16.1.
- **Fixtures:** `martin-ruiz-fixtures.json` (84 cases: aoi sweep −135…180 incl.
  89.999/90/90.001, `a_r` 0.01 and 5, 60 random in 0.08–0.25), generated by
  `scripts/fixtures/iam-martin-ruiz.py`.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-12`. Observed max error vs pvlib on V8: 1.1e-16; margin for cross-engine
  `Math.exp`/`Math.cos` ULP differences.
