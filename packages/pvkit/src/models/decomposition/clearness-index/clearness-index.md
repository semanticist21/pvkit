# clearness-index — global clearness index kt

## Principle

`kt = GHI / (I0 · max(cos z, minCosZenith))`, then clipped to `[0, maxClearnessIndex]`.
`minCosZenith` (default 0.065 ≈ 86.27°) keeps the horizontal extraterrestrial term from
vanishing near the horizon; negative GHI gives 0. Defaults match pvlib (0.065, 2); the
decomposition models call it with `maxClearnessIndex = 1`.

The file also holds the module-internal `spencerDniExtra(timeMs, solarConstant)` — Spencer
(1971) `I0 = S·(1.00011 + 0.034221 cos B + 0.00128 sin B + 0.000719 cos 2B + 7.7e-5 sin 2B)`,
`B = 2π(doy − 1)/365`, doy from the UTC date — used by erbs/boland (S = 1366.1) and
disc/dirint (S = 1370). It throws `RangeError` on a non-finite `timeMs`. Not exported from
the subpath entry.

## Reference

- **Spec:** B. Y. H. Liu, R. C. Jordan, "The interrelationship and characteristic
  distribution of direct, diffuse and total solar radiation", *Solar Energy* 4(3):1–19,
  1960, doi:10.1016/0038-092X(60)90062-1; cos-zenith floor per Maxwell (1987), SERI/TR-215-3087.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.irradiance.clearness_index` @ pvlib 0.16.1.
- **Fixtures:** `clearness-index-fixtures.json` (70 cases: zero/negative GHI, zenith 89–95°
  hitting the floor, clipping at 1 and 2, non-default floor; 60 random), generated by
  `scripts/fixtures/decomposition-clearness-index.py`.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-12`. Observed max error 2.2e-16 (1 ULP) — one cosine and one division.
