# ineichen — Ineichen–Perez clear-sky model

Clear-sky GHI, DNI and DHI from apparent zenith, absolute air mass and Linke turbidity.
Air mass and Linke turbidity are caller inputs (no lookup raster in pvkit).

## Principle

With altitude `h` (m), `fh1 = exp(−h/8000)`, `fh2 = exp(−h/1250)`,
`cg1 = 5.09e−5·h + 0.868`, `cg2 = 3.92e−5·h + 0.0387`, `AM` absolute air mass,
`TL` Linke turbidity, `I0` extraterrestrial normal irradiance:

1. `GHI = cg1 · I0 · cos z · exp(−cg2 · AM · (fh1 + fh2·(TL − 1)))`, optionally
   `× exp(0.01·AM^1.8)` (Perez enhancement, off by default — spurious near the horizon).
2. Beam `BncI = I0 · b · exp(−0.09 · AM · (TL − 1))`, `b = 0.664 + 0.163/fh1`
   (Ineichen & Perez 2002; Perez et al. 2002 would give `0.16268/fh1`).
3. Empirical cap `BncI₂ = GHI · (1 − (0.1 − 0.2·e^(−TL)) / (0.1 + 0.882/fh1)) / cos z`,
   clamped to `[0, 1e20]`; `DNI = min(BncI, BncI₂)`.
4. `DHI = GHI − DNI · cos z`.

Guards (pvlib behaviour): `cos z` floored at 0 → zeros at night; a NaN air mass (e.g.
from an air-mass model below the horizon) yields zeros, NaN zenith or Linke turbidity
propagate as NaN. RangeError for `linkeTurbidity ≤ 0` or `airmassAbsolute < 0`
(unphysical; pvlib would silently return garbage/NaN).

## Reference

- **Spec:** P. Ineichen, R. Perez, "A new airmass independent formulation for the Linke
  turbidity coefficient", *Solar Energy* 73(3):151–157, 2002,
  doi:10.1016/S0038-092X(02)00045-2; R. Perez et al., "A new operational model for
  satellite-derived irradiances: description and validation", *Solar Energy*
  73(5):307–317, 2002, doi:10.1016/S0038-092X(02)00122-6.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.clearsky.ineichen` @ pvlib 0.16.1, all arguments
  explicit.
- **Fixtures:** `ineichen-fixtures.json` (142 cases: zenith 0/30/60, near/below horizon
  89–120°, negative zenith, altitude −400 and 8848 m, TL 0.5–8, Perez enhancement on/off,
  100 random, 30 near-horizon zenith 85–90° with Kasten-Young air mass), generated by
  `scripts/fixtures/clearsky-ineichen.py`. No numeric worked example in the papers.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-10` W/m² absolute. Observed max error vs pvlib on V8: 3.4e-13 W/m²
  (relative 2.7e-15, ≈1–2 ULP). Same closed form in float64; margin covers cross-engine
  `Math.exp`/`Math.cos` differences.
