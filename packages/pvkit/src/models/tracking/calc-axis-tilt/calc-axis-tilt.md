# calcAxisTilt — tracker axis tilt on a sloped plane

Tilt from horizontal of a tracker axis that lies in a slope, from the slope's azimuth and
tilt and the axis azimuth.

## Principle

`tan βa = cos(γa − γg) · tan βg` (eqs. 18–19), with γg/βg the slope-normal azimuth and
slope tilt, γa the axis azimuth. Axis along the fall line → βa = ±βg; axis along the
contour → 0. Positive βa means the axis falls toward `axisAzimuth` (the `axisTilt`
convention of `singleaxis`). No guards: `slopeTilt` 90° gives ±90° via `atan` of a huge
value, exactly as pvlib.

## Reference

- **Spec:** K. Anderson, M. Mikofski, "Slope-Aware Backtracking for Single-Axis
  Trackers", NREL/TP-5K00-76626, 2020, doi:10.2172/1660126.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.tracking.calc_axis_tilt` @ pvlib 0.16.1.
- **Fixtures:** `calc-axis-tilt-fixtures.json` (100 cases: flat ground, axis along /
  against / across / oblique to the slope, slopes up to 89°, 90 random), generated by
  `scripts/fixtures/tracking-calc-axis-tilt.py`. Fall-line/contour identities also asserted.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-12` degrees. Observed max error vs pvlib on V8: ~7e-15° (one closed
  form, a few ULP of rounding).
