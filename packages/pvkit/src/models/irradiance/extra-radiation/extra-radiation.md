# extra-radiation — extraterrestrial normal irradiance

`E0 = Esc · (R0/R)²`, W/m², with solar constant `Esc` (default 1366.1 W/m²) and the
Earth–sun distance ratio from one of three methods.

## Principle

- **spencer** (default): day angle `B = 2π(doy − 1)/365`,
  `(R0/R)² = 1.00011 + 0.034221 cos B + 0.00128 sin B + 0.000719 cos 2B + 0.000077 sin 2B`.
- **asce**: `(R0/R)² = 1 + 0.033 cos(2π·doy/365)`.
- **nrel**: `(R0/R)² = R⁻²`, R (AU) from the NREL SPA radius vector
  `R = Σᵢ Rᵢ(JME)·JMEⁱ / 10⁸`, `Rᵢ = Σ A cos(B + C·JME)` (terms in `earth-radius-terms.ts`),
  `JME` from `timeMs` and `deltaT` (default 67 s) exactly as in SPA.

`doy` is the integer **UTC** day of year of `timeMs` (1..366) — like pvlib with a UTC
`Timestamp`; a local-time caller near midnight must pass the instant, not a local date.
pvlib's `pyephem` method is not offered: it needs the external PyEphem ephemeris, and
`nrel` is the more precise built-in alternative. `timeMs` must be finite (RangeError);
an unknown method throws RangeError.

The SPA R terms duplicate a slice of `solarposition/spa/spa-terms.ts` because modules may not
import each other.

## Reference

- **Spec:** J. W. Spencer, "Fourier series representation of the position of the sun",
  *Search* 2(5):172, 1971; ASCE, *The ASCE Standardized Reference Evapotranspiration
  Equation*, 2005, eq. 23, doi:10.1061/9780784408056; I. Reda, A. Andreas, NREL SPA,
  *Solar Energy* 76(5):577–589, 2004, doi:10.1016/j.solener.2003.12.003.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.irradiance.get_extra_radiation(pd.Timestamp(UTC),
  solar_constant, method)` @ pvlib 0.16.1; `method="nrel"` with `how="numpy", delta_t`.
- **Fixtures:** `extra-radiation-fixtures.json` (149 cases: per method, year start/end,
  perihelion, aphelion, leap day, 1900/2100; non-default solar constant / ΔT; 40 random
  instants × 3 methods), generated by `scripts/fixtures/irradiance-extra-radiation.py`.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-9` W/m². Observed max error vs pvlib: 0 (bit-identical). Closed forms
  in float64; nrel sums the same series in the same order.
