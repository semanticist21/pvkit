# poa-components — combine beam and diffuse into plane-of-array irradiance

## Principle

`poaDirect = max(dni · cos aoi, 0)` (beam is zero when the sun is behind the panel),
`poaDiffuse = poaSkyDiffuse + poaGroundDiffuse`, `poaGlobal = poaDirect + poaDiffuse`.
Sky-diffuse sub-components (pvlib dict input) are not supported.

## Reference

- **Spec:** J. A. Duffie, W. A. Beckman, *Solar Engineering of Thermal Processes*, 4th ed.,
  Wiley 2013, §2.15, doi:10.1002/9781118671603.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.irradiance.poa_components` @ pvlib 0.16.1.
- **Fixtures:** `poa-components-fixtures.json` (60 random cases, aoi 0–180°), generated by
  `scripts/fixtures/irradiance-total-irradiance.py`.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-12` relative to `max(1, |expected|)` W/m². Observed max error: 1.5e-16 (relative; non-finite outcomes match exactly).
