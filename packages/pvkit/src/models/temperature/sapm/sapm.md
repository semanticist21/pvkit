# sapm — Sandia Array Performance Model temperature

Steady-state back-surface and cell temperature from POA irradiance, air temperature and
10 m wind (King et al. 2004). Three functions: `sapmModule`, `sapmCellFromModule`,
`sapmCell` (= the two chained), plus `SAPM_TEMPERATURE_PARAMETERS` presets.

## Principle

- Back-surface (eq. 11): `Tm = E·exp(a + b·WS) + Ta`.
- Cell (eq. 12): `Tc = Tm + (E / E₀)·ΔT`, `E₀ = irradRef` (default 1000 W/m²).

`a` sets the low-wind upper limit, `b` (< 0) the cooling rate with wind; `ΔT` (`tempDelta`) is the
cell–back difference at E₀. Presets (`sapm-parameters.ts`, King Table 1): open-rack and
close-mount glass/glass, open-rack glass/polymer, insulated-back glass/polymer.

No guards: closed form, NaN propagates. Negative irradiance is computed as given (pvlib
does the same).

## Reference

- **Spec:** D. L. King, W. E. Boyson, J. A. Kratochvil, "Photovoltaic Array Performance
  Model", SAND2004-3535, Sandia National Laboratories, 2004, doi:10.2172/919131.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.temperature.sapm_cell`, `sapm_module` @ pvlib
  0.16.1 (`irrad_ref` passed explicitly); presets from
  `pvlib.temperature.TEMPERATURE_MODEL_PARAMETERS["sapm"]`, extracted by the fixture script.
- **Fixtures:** `sapm-fixtures.json` (132 cases: every preset × {1000 W/m² calm/1 m/s, 0
  and −5 W/m², 1400 W/m² at 50 °C, −40 °C at 30 m/s, E₀ = 800}, 100 random over
  E 0–1400, Ta −30–50, WS 0–20, random a/b/ΔT/E₀), generated by
  `scripts/fixtures/temperature-sapm.py`. `sapmCellFromModule` is checked with pvlib's
  module temperature as input. Also asserts pvlib's docstring value 44.11703066106086 °C.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-12` °C. Observed max error ~7e-15 °C on V8. One `exp`, a few flops in
  float64; the margin covers 1–2 ULP `Math.exp` differences across engines.
