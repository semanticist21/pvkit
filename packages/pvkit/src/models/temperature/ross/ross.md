# ross — Ross NOCT cell temperature

## Principle

`Tc = Ta + (NOCT − 20)/80 · E·0.1` — linear rise with irradiance, scaled so the cell is at
NOCT under NOCT conditions (800 W/m², 20 °C). The `0.1` converts W/m² to the paper's
mW/cm². Only the `noct` form is implemented; pvlib's alternative direct-coefficient `k`
form (`Tc = Ta + k·E`) is one multiplication the caller can do. No wind term. No guards.

## Reference

- **Spec:** R. G. Ross Jr., "Design Techniques for Flat-Plate Photovoltaic Arrays", 15th
  IEEE Photovoltaic Specialists Conference, Orlando, 1981; form as surveyed in E. Skoplaki,
  J. A. Palyvos, *Renewable Energy* 34(1):23–29, 2009, doi:10.1016/j.renene.2008.04.009.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.temperature.ross(noct=...)` @ pvlib 0.16.1.
- **Fixtures:** `ross-fixtures.json` (67 cases: NOCT conditions, 0 / −5 W/m², 1400 W/m² at
  50 °C, NOCT 20 (no rise), 60 random), generated by `scripts/fixtures/temperature-ross.py`.
  Also asserts `Tc = NOCT` at NOCT conditions.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-12` °C. Observed max error 0 (bit-identical on V8). Pure arithmetic in float64.
