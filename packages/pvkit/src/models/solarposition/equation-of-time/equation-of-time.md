# equation-of-time — simple day-of-year forms

Equation of time `E` (apparent − mean solar time), minutes, from the day of year `n` alone.
Use when SPA is overkill; `spa` returns a precise `equationOfTime` per instant.

## Principle

Day angle `Γ = 2π(n − 1)/365` (radians).

- **Spencer (1971)** — `equationOfTimeSpencer71`:
  `E = (1440/2π)·(0.0000075 + 0.001868 cos Γ − 0.032077 sin Γ − 0.014615 cos 2Γ − 0.040849 sin 2Γ)`.
  Constant 0.0000075 (not the misprinted 0.000075) and 0.040849 (not 0.04089) per the
  author-corrected reprint, as in pvlib. Max error ~0.5 min vs. ephemeris.
- **PVCDROM / Kalogirou (2009)** — `equationOfTimePvcdrom`:
  `B = Γ − 2π·80/365 = 2π(n − 81)/365`, `E = 9.87 sin 2B − 7.53 cos B − 1.5 sin B`.

No validation: `n` may be fractional or outside 1..366 (the forms are 365-day periodic).

## Reference

- **Spec:** J. W. Spencer, "Fourier series representation of the position of the sun",
  *Search* 2(5):172, 1971 (corrected reprint: http://www.mail-archive.com/sundial@uni-koeln.de/msg01050.html);
  S. A. Kalogirou, *Solar Energy Engineering*, 2nd ed., Academic Press, 2009 (via
  https://www.pveducation.org/pvcdrom/properties-of-sunlight/solar-time).
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.solarposition.equation_of_time_spencer71`,
  `equation_of_time_pvcdrom` @ pvlib 0.16.1.
- **Fixtures:** `equation-of-time-fixtures.json` (74 cases: days 0/1/59/60/81/172/264/355/
  365/366/367, fractional days, 60 random), generated by
  `scripts/fixtures/solarposition-equation-of-time.py`. Sanity check against the known
  annual extremes (≈ −14.2 min day 42, ≈ +16.4 min day 307).
- **Tolerance:** `1e-12` min. Observed max error ~2e-15 min (same closed form in float64,
  1–2 ULP of `Math.sin/cos`).
