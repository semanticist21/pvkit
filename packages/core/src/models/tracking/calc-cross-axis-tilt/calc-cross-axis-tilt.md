# calcCrossAxisTilt — cross-axis slope tilt

Angle from horizontal of the line where the slope containing the tracker axes meets a plane
perpendicular to the axes. Input to `singleaxis({ crossAxisTilt })` for slope-aware
backtracking.

## Principle

With Δγ = γa − γg, βa the axis tilt and βg the slope tilt:

1. Tracker normal (eq. 22): `v = (sin Δγ cos βa cos βg, sin βa sin βg + cos Δγ cos βa
   cos βg, −sin Δγ sin βg cos βa)`.
2. Cross-axis tilt (eq. 26): `βc = asin(((vx cos Δγ − vy sin Δγ) sin βa + vz cos βa) / |v|)`.

Right-handed sign: for axes heading south, βc < 0 when the slope falls to the east, > 0 to
the west. `axisTilt` is normally `calcAxisTilt` of the same slope; no clipping of the
`asin` argument, as pvlib.

## Reference

- **Spec:** K. Anderson, M. Mikofski, "Slope-Aware Backtracking for Single-Axis
  Trackers", NREL/TP-5K00-76626, 2020, doi:10.2172/1660126.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.tracking.calc_cross_axis_tilt` @ pvlib 0.16.1, with
  `axis_tilt` from `pvlib.tracking.calc_axis_tilt`.
- **Fixtures:** `calc-cross-axis-tilt-fixtures.json` (100 cases, same slope geometry as
  `calc-axis-tilt`), generated by `scripts/fixtures/tracking-calc-axis-tilt.py`. East/west/
  along-axis sign identities also asserted.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-12` degrees. Observed max error vs pvlib on V8: ~7e-15°.
