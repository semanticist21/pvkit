# singleaxis — single-axis tracker rotation with backtracking

Rotation angle, surface orientation and angle of incidence of a single-axis tracker for one
sun position. Slope-aware backtracking avoids row-to-row shading.

## Principle

Tracker frame: +y along the axis (`axisAzimuth`, tilted down by `axisTilt`), +x 90°
clockwise from +y and horizontal, +z skyward. Rotation θ is right-handed about +y
(axis heading south: θ > 0 faces west).

1. Sun vector `s = (sin z sin γs, sin z cos γs, cos z)` (E, N, Up), with z the apparent
   zenith.
2. True tracking (eqs. 4–5): `s′x = sx cos γa − sy sin γa`,
   `s′z = sx sin γa sin βa + sy sin βa cos γa + sz cos βa`, `ωideal = atan2(s′x, s′z)`.
3. Backtracking (eqs. 14–16): `T = |cos(ωideal − βc) / (gcr cos βc)|`; if `T < 1`,
   `θ = ωideal − sign(ωideal)·acos T`, otherwise `θ = ωideal` (no shade to avoid). The
   absolute value handles suns below the array plane (pvlib GH 824).
4. Clip θ to `[minAngle, maxAngle]` (default `minAngle = −maxAngle`).
5. Surface tilt `βs = acos(cos θ cos βa)` (Marion & Dobos eq. 1). Surface azimuth from the
   rotated unit normal `Rz(−γa) Rx(−βa) Ry(θ) ẑ`: `atan2(nx, ny)`, wrapped to [0, 360);
   when the surface is exactly flat (`cos θ cos βa` rounds to 1) it is pinned to
   `axisAzimuth − 90`, as pvlib does.
6. `aoi = acos(clip(cos βs cos z + sin βs sin z cos(γs − γsurf), −1, 1))`.

Guards (mirroring pvlib): `apparentZenith > 90` → all four outputs `NaN`; zenith exactly
90° is still tracked. `NaN` inputs propagate to `NaN` outputs. `RangeError` when
`minAngle > maxAngle` (incl. negative `maxAngle`) or, with `backtrack`, `gcr ∉ (0, 1]`
(pvlib documents `gcr ≤ 1` but does not check). pvlib's tuple `max_angle=(min, max)` is
the `minAngle`/`maxAngle` pair here. The true-tracking step is pvlib
`shading.projected_solar_zenith_angle`; it stays internal (not a tracking method).

## Reference

- **Spec:** K. Anderson, M. Mikofski, "Slope-Aware Backtracking for Single-Axis
  Trackers", NREL/TP-5K00-76626, 2020, doi:10.2172/1660126; E. Lorenzo, L. Narvarte,
  J. Muñoz, "Tracking and back-tracking", *Prog. Photovolt.* 19(6):747–753, 2011,
  doi:10.1002/pip.1085; W. Marion, A. Dobos, "Rotation Angle for the Optimum Tracking of
  One-Axis Trackers", NREL/TP-6A20-58891, 2013, doi:10.2172/1089596.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.tracking.singleaxis` @ pvlib 0.16.1, every
  argument passed explicitly (`max_angle=(min, max)` when asymmetric).
- **Fixtures:** `singleaxis-fixtures.json` (154 cases: solar noon / flat sentinel, zenith
  0, 90, 90.001, 120, `maxAngle` 0, asymmetric limits, `gcr` 1, sloped backtracking, 100
  random incl. below-horizon, 40 near-horizon zenith 85–90°; `null` = `NaN`), generated by
  `scripts/fixtures/tracking-singleaxis.py`. Also asserted: closed-form true tracking of
  an eastern sun and the Lorenzo flat-ground backtracking angle.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-9` degrees. Observed max error vs pvlib on V8: ~6e-14° for θ, tilt
  and azimuth, ~2e-13° for aoi. Same formulas in float64; aoi is ill-conditioned near 0°
  (`acos` near 1 turns a 1-ULP projection change into up to ~1e-6°), so a perfectly
  sun-facing case can exceed 1e-9 across engines — the fixture set has none.
