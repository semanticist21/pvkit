# fuentes — Fuentes transient heat-balance module temperature

First-principles energy balance with thermal mass (Fuentes 1987), used by PVWatts.
**Step function:** one call advances one timestep; the caller iterates and carries the
state (`prevTempModule`, `prevPoaGlobal`) — see the `@example` in `fuentes.ts`.

## Principle

1. Calibrate at installed NOCT `T_N` (800 W/m², 20 °C air, 1 m/s, sky 282.21 K): top-side
   convection `h_c(T_N)`, back/ground ratio → ground temperature `T_g` clipped to
   [293.15, T_N] → `tgrat = (T_g − 293.15)/(T_N − 293.15)` and total-to-top convection
   ratio `convrat`. Heat capacity `C = 11 000 J/(m²·K)`, × `1 + (T_N − 321.15)/12` when
   `T_N > 321.15 K` (eqs. 26–27).
2. Per step: sky `T_sky = 0.68·0.0552·T_a^1.5 + 0.32·T_a` (eq. 24), wind at module height
   `v = WS·(h_mod/h_wind)^0.2 + 1e-4` (eq. 22), absorbed `S = α·E`.
3. Convection `h_c = convrat·(h_free³ + h_forced³)^(1/3)`: free via Grashof
   (`∝ sin tilt`), forced laminar, turbulent when Re > 1.2e5. Radiation
   `h_sky = εσ(T² + T_sky²)(T + T_sky)`, `h_g = εσ(T² + T_g²)(T + T_g)`,
   `T_g = T_a + tgrat·(T − T_a)` (eqs. 3–4).
4. Lag `λ = −(h_c + h_sky + h_g)·Δt / C` (eq. 8); `e = exp λ` (0 if λ ≤ −10).
   `T = T₀·e + [(1 − e)(h_c T_a + h_sky T_sky + h_g T_g + S₀ + (S − S₀)/λ) + S − S₀] / Σh`
   (eq. 7). Losses depend on `T` → 10 fixed-point passes starting at `T₀`, as the FORTRAN.

σ = 5.669e-8 as printed in the paper. Guards: `noctInstalled > 20` (the calibration
divides by `T_N − 293.15`) and finite `timestepSeconds > 0`, else `RangeError`.

**Initial state:** pvlib starts from `T₀ = 20 °C`, `S₀ = 0`, and reuses the second
interval as the first `Δt`; pass the same to reproduce it. Like pvlib (and unlike SAM
SSC) night steps are integrated, not snapped to ambient. The NOCT calibration is
recomputed every call (cheap; keeps the step pure).

## Reference

- **Spec:** M. K. Fuentes, "A Simplified Thermal Model for Flat-Plate Photovoltaic Arrays",
  SAND85-0330, Sandia National Laboratories, 1987 (FORTRAN in Appendix A),
  http://prod.sandia.gov/techlib/access-control.cgi/1985/850330.pdf; PVWatts use: A. P. Dobos, "PVWatts Version 5
  Manual", NREL/TP-6A20-62641, 2014, doi:10.2172/1158421.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.temperature.fuentes` @ pvlib 0.16.1 over pandas
  series, all keyword arguments explicit.
- **Fixtures:** `fuentes-fixtures.json` (144 steps in 4 series, tagged `series`, each with
  the per-step inputs and pvlib's prior state: two hourly days at NOCT 45; 5-min cloudy
  midday at NOCT 49 with calm steps (heavier capacity); irregular 1 min–3 h steps with
  custom geometry/optics at NOCT 55 and sub-zero air; night-to-dawn gale (turbulent
  branch) at tilt 60°), generated by `scripts/fixtures/temperature-fuentes.py`. Tests
  check each step with pvlib's prior state and the chained run with pvkit's own state;
  also that the module settles at NOCT under NOCT conditions (±0.05 °C).
- **Tolerance:** `1e-12` °C (~9× margin, ~17 ulp at 300 K). Observed max error ~1.1e-13 °C on V8 (per step / chained). Same
  formulas in float64; `Math.cbrt` vs `**(1/3)`, K↔°C round trips and seconds vs
  hours·3600 perturb low bits, and 10 nested iterations plus chaining amplify them mildly.
