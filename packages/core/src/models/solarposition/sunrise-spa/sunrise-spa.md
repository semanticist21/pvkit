# sunrise-spa — sunrise, sunset, transit (SPA)

Sun transit, sunrise and sunset for one day, as UTC epoch ms, ~seconds accuracy.

## Principle

SPA Appendix A.2. Day `D` = 00:00 UTC of `timeMs` (floored).

1. Apparent sidereal time `ν` at 0h UT of `D`; geocentric `α`, `δ` (SPA §3.1–3.10, private
   `spa/spa-geocentric.ts`) at 0h TT of `D−1`, `D`, `D+1`.
2. Approximate transit `m0 = (α0 − λ − ν)/360`; local hour angle at rise/set
   `H0 = acos((sin h0′ − sin φ sin δ0)/(cos φ cos δ0))`, `h0′ = −0.8333°` (sun radius +
   refraction). `m_T = m0 mod 1`, `m_R,S = (m_T ∓ H0/360) mod 1`.
3. For each event: `ν_i = ν + 360.985647·m_i`, `n_i = m_i + ΔT/86400`; interpolate
   `α′, δ′ = X0 + n(a + b + c·n)/2` (day-to-day differences `a, b` wrapped to [0, 1) when
   |·| > 2); local hour angle `H′ = ν_i + λ − α′` in [−180, 180); altitude `h′`.
4. Transit `T = m_T − H′_T/360`; rise/set `m + (h′ − h0′)/(360 cos δ′ cos φ sin H′)`
   (fraction of day). As in pvlib, sunrise is moved one day back if `m_T − H0/360 < 0`
   and sunset one day forward if `m_T + H0/360 ≥ 1`, so they can fall outside `D`.

Python-style modulo (`((x % m) + m) % m`) mirrors the reference's `%` on negatives.

**Polar day/night:** if `|cos H0| > 1`, `sunrise` and `sunset` are `NaN` (pvlib: `NaT`);
`transit` is still returned. **Local days:** pvlib relabels the local calendar date's
midnight as UTC; do the same by passing `Date.UTC(y, m, d)` for that local date.

## Reference

- **Spec:** I. Reda, A. Andreas, NREL/TP-560-34302 (rev. 2008), Appendix A.2,
  https://www.nrel.gov/docs/fy08osti/34302.pdf; *Solar Energy* 76(5):577–589, 2004,
  doi:10.1016/j.solener.2003.12.003.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.solarposition.sun_rise_set_transit_spa(how="numpy")`
  @ pvlib 0.16.1, UTC-localized times (pvlib normalizes to the day), ΔT passed explicitly.
- **Fixtures:** `sunrise-spa-fixtures.json` (125 cases: paper example day, leap day,
  solstices/equinoxes, ±180° longitude, Svalbard polar day and night, threshold latitudes,
  1900/2100, two non-midnight inputs (one pre-1970), 80 random |φ| ≤ 70°, 30 random
  60° ≤ |φ| ≤ 89.5°), generated by
  `scripts/fixtures/solarposition-sunrise-spa.py`. The paper example (sunrise 06:12:43,
  transit 11:46:04, sunset 17:20:19 MST) is also asserted to within 1 s.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-2` ms. Observed max error ~5e-4 ms: the epoch-ms result (~1.7e12)
  has a ULP of ~2.4e-4 ms and pvlib rounds to whole ns; near-polar cases amplify through
  `1/sin H′`.
