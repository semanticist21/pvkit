# spa — NREL Solar Position Algorithm

Topocentric solar zenith, azimuth, elevation and equation of time for one instant and
one observer. Stated accuracy ±0.0003° for years −2000 to 6000 (paper).

## Principle

1. Julian day from `timeMs` (`JD = timeMs / 86 400 000 + 2 440 587.5`), ephemeris day
   `JDE = JD + ΔT / 86 400`; centuries `JC`, `JCE`, millennium `JME = JCE / 10`.
2. Earth heliocentric longitude `L`, latitude `B`, radius `R` from the VSOP87 periodic
   terms (`spa-terms.ts`): `Xᵢ = Σ A·cos(B + C·JME)`, `X = Σ Xᵢ·JMEⁱ / 10⁸`.
3. Geocentric `Θ = L + 180°`, `β = −B`.
4. Nutation `Δψ`, `Δε` from the five fundamental arguments X0..X4 and 63 terms.
5. True obliquity `ε = ε0/3600 + Δε` (ε0: 10th-order polynomial in `U = JME/10`).
6. Apparent longitude `λ = Θ + Δψ − 20.4898″ / (3600·R)`.
7. Apparent sidereal time `ν = ν0 + Δψ·cos ε`; geocentric `α`, `δ`.
8. Hour angle `H = ν + longitude − α`; parallax (`ξ = 8.794″ / R`, flattening
   0.99664719, Earth radius 6 378 140 m) → topocentric `δ′`, `H′`.
9. Elevation `e0 = asin(sin φ sin δ′ + cos φ cos δ′ cos H′)`; refraction
   `Δe = (P/1010)(283/(273+T))·1.02 / (60·tan(e0 + 10.3/(e0 + 5.11)))`, applied only when
   `e0 ≥ −(0.26667° + 0.5667°)` (sun radius + standard refraction at the horizon).
10. Azimuth `Φ = atan2(sin H′, cos H′ sin φ − tan δ′ cos φ) + 180°` (north, clockwise).
11. Equation of time `E = 4·(M − 0.0057183 − α + Δψ·cos ε)` minutes, wrapped to (−20, 20].

Every accumulated angle goes through `limitDegrees` before trig (paper's `limit_degrees`).
Observer `altitude` (m) feeds the parallax in step 8; `pressure` (Pa, converted to mbar) and
`tempAir` (°C) are `P` and `T` in step 9. Refraction constant fixed at
0.5667° (paper default). `deltaT` defaults to 67 s, the paper's example value — at PV-era
dates the difference to the true ΔT (~69 s) moves the sun by ~1e-5°.

Steps 1–7 live in the private `spa-geocentric.ts`, shared with `sunrise-spa` and
`earth-sun-distance`. Not implemented: incidence on a tilted surface, ΔUT1 input.

## Reference

- **Spec:** I. Reda, A. Andreas, "Solar position algorithm for solar radiation
  applications", *Solar Energy* 76(5):577–589, 2004, doi:10.1016/j.solener.2003.12.003;
  corrigendum *Solar Energy* 81(6):838, 2007. Full text: NREL/TP-560-34302 (rev. 2008),
  https://www.nrel.gov/docs/fy08osti/34302.pdf.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.solarposition.spa_python(how="numpy",
  atmos_refract=0.5667)` @ pvlib 0.16.1, ΔT passed explicitly.
- **Fixtures:** `spa-fixtures.json` (168 cases: paper Table A5.1 example, leap day,
  1900/2100, poles, dateline, 120 random, 40 near-horizon zenith 84–96°), generated by
  `scripts/fixtures/solarposition-spa.py`. The paper example is also asserted against the
  published values (zenith 50.11162°, azimuth 194.34024°).
- **Tolerance:** `1e-9` (degrees; minutes for EoT). Observed max error vs pvlib on V8:
  ~1e-13° for angles, ~3e-11 min for EoT (the ×4 and the large mean-longitude term amplify
  rounding). Same formulas in float64, so the gap is pure rounding; the margin absorbs
  1–2 ULP `Math.*` differences across engines (JSC, Hermes).
