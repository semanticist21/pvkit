# earth-sun-distance — Earth–Sun distance (SPA)

Earth–Sun distance in AU at one instant — the SPA heliocentric radius vector `R`.

## Principle

Julian ephemeris millennium `JME` from `timeMs` and `ΔT` (as in `spa`), then
`R = Σᵢ (Σ A·cos(B + C·JME))·JMEⁱ / 10⁸` over the VSOP87 radius terms R0..R4
(`spa/spa-terms.ts`, shared through the private `spa/spa-geocentric.ts`). `deltaT`
defaults to 67 s; its effect on `R` is ~1e-9 AU.

## Reference

- **Spec:** I. Reda, A. Andreas, "Solar position algorithm for solar radiation
  applications", *Solar Energy* 76(5):577–589, 2004, doi:10.1016/j.solener.2003.12.003;
  NREL/TP-560-34302 (rev. 2008) §3.2, https://www.nrel.gov/docs/fy08osti/34302.pdf.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.solarposition.nrel_earthsun_distance(how="numpy")`
  @ pvlib 0.16.1, ΔT passed explicitly.
- **Fixtures:** `earth-sun-distance-fixtures.json` (60 cases: paper example instant,
  perihelion/aphelion 2025, leap day, 1900/2100, 54 random), generated by
  `scripts/fixtures/solarposition-earth-sun-distance.py`. The paper example is also asserted
  against the published `R = 0.9965422974` AU.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-12` AU. Observed max error 0 (bit-identical on V8) (same series in float64).
