# declination — simple day-of-year forms

Solar declination `δ`, degrees, from the day of year `n` alone. pvlib returns radians;
pvkit returns branded `Degrees`.

## Principle

Day angle `Γ = 2π(n − 1)/365` (radians).

- **Spencer (1971)** — `declinationSpencer71` (max error ~0.035°):
  `δ = 0.006918 − 0.399912 cos Γ + 0.070257 sin Γ − 0.006758 cos 2Γ + 0.000907 sin 2Γ
  − 0.002697 cos 3Γ + 0.00148 sin 3Γ` (radians) → degrees.
- **Cooper (1969)** — `declinationCooper69`: `δ = 23.45°·sin(2π(284 + n)/365)`, written as
  `sin(Γ + 2π·285/365)` like pvlib.

No validation: `n` may be fractional or outside 1..366 (the forms are 365-day periodic).

## Reference

- **Spec:** J. W. Spencer, "Fourier series representation of the position of the sun",
  *Search* 2(5):172, 1971; P. I. Cooper, "The absorption of radiation in solar stills",
  *Solar Energy* 12(3):333–346, 1969, doi:10.1016/0038-092X(69)90047-4; both as given in
  J. A. Duffie, W. A. Beckman, *Solar Engineering of Thermal Processes*, 3rd ed., 2006, pp. 13–14.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.solarposition.declination_spencer71`,
  `declination_cooper69` @ pvlib 0.16.1 (outputs converted with `np.degrees`).
- **Fixtures:** `declination-fixtures.json` (74 cases, same day mix as equation-of-time),
  generated by `scripts/fixtures/solarposition-declination.py`. Sanity check: δ ≈ 23.45° at
  the June solstice (n = 172).
- **Tolerance:** `1e-12`°. Observed max error ~4e-15° (rad→deg rounding only).
