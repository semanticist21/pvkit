# hour-angle — hour angle in local solar time

Hour angle `ω`, degrees, zero at local solar noon, negative in the morning.

## Principle

`ω = 15·(t_UTC − 12) + λ + E/4`, with `t_UTC` the UTC hour of day of `timeMs`, `λ` the
east-positive longitude and `E` the equation of time in minutes (caller input — from
`equation-of-time` or `spa`). The result is wrapped to [−180°, 180°) so the sign reliably
marks morning/afternoon (needed by the analytical azimuth).

**Deviation from pvlib:** `pvlib.solarposition.hour_angle` takes tz-localized times and
does not wrap, so its value depends on the time zone by multiples of 360°. pvkit takes
UTC `timeMs` and wraps; the two agree modulo 360°.

## Reference

- **Spec:** J. A. Duffie, W. A. Beckman, *Solar Engineering of Thermal Processes*, 3rd ed.,
  Wiley, 2006, p. 13.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.solarposition.hour_angle` @ pvlib 0.16.1 with a
  UTC-localized `DatetimeIndex`.
- **Fixtures:** `hour-angle-fixtures.json` (66 cases: UTC noon at Greenwich, leap day,
  ±180° longitude, day boundaries 00:00/23:59:59, 1969/2100, 60 random), generated by
  `scripts/fixtures/solarposition-hour-angle.py`. Compared modulo 360°.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-9`°. Observed max error ~6e-14° (pvlib derives the hour from ns
  timestamps, pvkit from ms; both exact, so only the final sum rounds).
