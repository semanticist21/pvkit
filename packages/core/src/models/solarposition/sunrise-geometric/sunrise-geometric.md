# sunrise-geometric — sunrise, sunset, transit (geometric)

Quick sunrise/sunset/transit from declination and equation of time, as UTC epoch ms.
Point-source sun, circular orbit, no refraction: error of order 10 minutes. Use
`sunrise-spa` for accuracy.

## Principle

Day `D` = 00:00 UTC of `timeMs` (floored). Sunset hour angle
`ωs = acos(−tan δ · tan φ)`; event hour angles `−ωs`, `ωs`, `0` map to UTC hours
`(ω − λ − E/4)/15 + 12` after `D`, i.e. the inverse of `hour-angle`. `δ` (degrees) and `E`
(minutes) are caller inputs, e.g. `declinationSpencer71` / `equationOfTimeSpencer71`.
Events can fall outside `D` for large |λ|.

**Polar day/night:** `|tan δ tan φ| > 1` → `acos` is `NaN` → `sunrise`/`sunset` are
`NaN` (pvlib: `NaT`); `transit` is still returned. Declination is degrees here (pvlib:
radians).

## Reference

- **Spec:** J. A. Duffie, W. A. Beckman, *Solar Engineering of Thermal Processes*, 3rd ed.,
  Wiley, 2006, §1.6; F. Vignola et al., *Solar and Infrared Radiation Measurements*, CRC
  Press, 2012.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.solarposition.sun_rise_set_transit_geometric`
  @ pvlib 0.16.1, UTC-localized times, declination passed as `np.radians(δ)`.
- **Fixtures:** `sunrise-geometric-fixtures.json` (79 cases: 4 days with Spencer δ/E incl.
  leap day and ±180° longitude, equinox δ = 0, polar day/night, Arctic-circle threshold,
  70 random), generated by `scripts/fixtures/solarposition-sunrise-geometric.py`.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-2` ms. Observed max error ~5e-4 ms (epoch-ms ULP ~2.4e-4 ms; pvlib
  rounds to whole ns).
