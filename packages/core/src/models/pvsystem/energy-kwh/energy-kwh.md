# energy-kwh — energy from a power series

Integrates a regularly sampled power series to energy.

## Principle

`E = Σ P_i · Δt / 1000` [kWh], `Δt = stepHours`. The sum is Neumaier-compensated
(`compensatedSum`, `src/sum.ts`) — a naive `+=` over a year of minutes drifts by ~2e-14
relative on the fixture series (see `doc/architecture.md` → "Numerical strategy").
Rectangle rule: each value stands for its whole step (mean power, or an instantaneous
sample). Values are taken as given — negative night tare reduces energy, NaN propagates.
`stepHours` must be finite and > 0, otherwise `RangeError`.

## Reference

- **Spec:** rectangle-rule integration; Neumaier, "Rundungsfehleranalyse einiger Verfahren
  zur Summation endlicher Summen", *ZAMM* 54(1):39–51, 1974, doi:10.1002/zamm.19740540106.
- **Reference implementation:** `math.fsum(power) * step_hours / 1000` (Python 3.11, exactly
  rounded sum); pvlib 0.16.1 has no energy integrator.
- **Fixtures:** `energy-kwh-fixtures.json`, generated by
  `scripts/fixtures/pvsystem-energy-kwh.py`: 66 small cases (empty, zeros, 1 kW·1 h,
  negative tare, a `1e16, 1, −1e16` cancellation, 60 random series of 1–99 values at 1-min
  to 1-h steps) plus `meta.year`: 525 600 minute values from an xorshift32 stream
  (`x/2³²·scale`, daylight minutes 360–1079), regenerated bit-identically by the test
  instead of being stored.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-15` relative. Observed max error on V8: 0 (year and small cases). The
  test also asserts that the naive sum's drift (`meta.year.naiveEnergyKwh`, 1.9e-14) exceeds
  the tolerance, so it would catch a non-compensated sum.
