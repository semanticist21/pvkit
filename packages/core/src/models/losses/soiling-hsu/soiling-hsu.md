# soiling-hsu — HSU fixed-velocity soiling ratio (one step)

Soiling ratio (1 − transmission loss) from particulate deposition and rain cleaning.

## Principle

Per step ending at `t` with duration `Δt` (s):

1. Cleaning: `R_window(t) ≥ cleaningThreshold`, with `R_window` the rainfall over the
   trailing window `(t − rain_accum_period, t]` (pvlib default 1 h; paper suggests 1–24 h).
2. Deposited mass rate on the tilted module (gravitational settling only):
   `ṁ = (PM2.5·v2.5 + max(PM10 − PM2.5, 0)·v10) · Δt · cos(tilt)`, v2.5 = 0.0009 m/s,
   v10 = 0.004 m/s by default.
3. `m(t) = 0` on cleaning, else `m(t) = m(t − Δt) + ṁ` (g/m²).
4. `SR = 1 − 0.3437 · erf(0.17 · m^0.8473)`; minimum ≈ 0.6563, ≈ 0.6875 at the paper's
   validated 10 g/m².

`erf` has no `Math` builtin; it uses the all-positive series (Abramowitz & Stegun 7.1.6),
which has no cancellation (a few ULP), returning 1 for x ≥ 6 where erfc < ε/2.

## Caller iteration

State in/out: `prevAccumulatedMass`/`accumulatedMass` (start 0). The caller computes the
trailing-window rainfall sum. pvlib uses each step's own interval and assumes the interval
before the first sample equals the first — pass that `timestepMs` on step 0. PM inputs may
vary per step.

A NaN window sum does not clean (pvlib compares `>=`, false for NaN).

Guards: `RangeError` for negative/non-finite `timestepMs`, `surfaceTilt` outside [0, 90]
(pvlib returns NaN past 90° and the next step then fails), negative PM or prior mass (which
would make `m^0.8473` NaN).

## Reference

- **Spec:** M. Coello, L. Boyle, "Simple Model For Predicting Time Series Soiling of
  Photovoltaic Panels", *IEEE J. Photovoltaics* 9(5):1382–1387, 2019,
  doi:10.1109/JPHOTOV.2019.2919628.
- **Reference implementation:** `pvlib.soiling.hsu` @ pvlib 0.16.1 with `depo_veloc` and
  `rain_accum_period` passed explicitly.
- **Fixtures:** `soiling-hsu-fixtures.json` — 6 series, 792 steps (urban PM at 30°; heavy
  dust, horizontal, 24 h window approaching the floor, min SR 0.6566; per-step PM with
  PM10 < PM2.5; vertical module; irregular timestamps with custom velocities and 3 h
  window; rain exactly at the threshold incl. step 0), generated by
  `scripts/fixtures/losses-soiling-hsu.py`. The test chains the step function per series.
  Paper/pvlib-doc anchors (floor 0.6563, ≈0.6875 at 10 g/m²) are asserted too.
- **Tolerance:** `1e-12` absolute. Observed max error vs pvlib (scipy `erf`) on V8:
  2.2e-16. Incremental mass vs pvlib's `cumsum − cumsum at cleaning` and the series erf vs
  Cephes differ by a few ULP; margin covers cross-engine `Math.exp`/`**` noise.
